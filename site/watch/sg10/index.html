Sales Guide: Rare Disease: Neurodevelopmental & Syndromic

Sales team test guide: 7 tests in Rare Disease: Neurodevelopmental & Syndromic. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Paediatricians, paediatric neurologists, developmental paediatricians, cardiologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Paediatricians, paediatric neurologists, developmental paediatricians, cardiologists
Pitch tip
Unclear syndrome? Microarray first, then exome. Specific signs: targeted test.
Fragile-X Syndrome (FMR1 repeat expansion)
What: Counts FMR1 CGG repeats.
When: Learning difficulty or autism, especially in boys; family history.
Sales pitch: "Exome can miss it: order it by name."
Code PTMP0078 · 18 days · TP-PCR · EDTA whole blood
DiGeorge Syndrome (22q11.2 microdeletion) by FISH
What: Detects the 22q11.2 deletion.
When: Heart defect, cleft palate, low calcium, frequent infections.
Sales pitch: "Heart defect plus low calcium: check 22q11."
Code PTMP0177 · 10 days · FISH · Heparin blood / amniotic fluid
Williams Syndrome (7q11.23 microdeletion) by FISH
What: Detects the 7q11.23 deletion.
When: Typical face, narrowed aorta, very friendly nature, high calcium.
Sales pitch: "A 5-day answer for a typical picture."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 5 days · FISH · Heparin blood / amniotic fluid
Prader-Willi / Angelman Syndrome by MS-MLPA
What: Methylation test for 15q11-q13.
When: PWS: floppy newborn, later overeating. AS: severe delay, no speech, happy.
Sales pitch: "One test covers both syndromes."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 20 days · MS-MLPA · EDTA whole blood
Beckwith-Wiedemann / Russell-Silver Syndrome (MS-MLPA)
What: Methylation test for 11p15.
When: BWS: overgrowth, big tongue, low sugar. RSS: poor growth.
Sales pitch: "Growth too much or too little: test 11p15."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · MS-MLPA · EDTA blood
Rett Syndrome (MECP2) Deletion / Duplication
What: MECP2 deletions and duplications.
When: Girl losing skills, hand-wringing; MECP2 duplication in boys.
Sales pitch: "About 1 in 10 Rett changes need MLPA to find."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · MLPA · EDTA blood
Rett Syndrome Gene Panel
What: Sequencing of Rett-related genes (MECP2, CDKL5, FOXG1).
When: Classic or atypical Rett picture.
Sales pitch: "Covers atypical Rett genes too."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · EDTA blood
