Sales Guide: Pharmacogenomics

Sales team test guide: 4 tests in Pharmacogenomics. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Psychiatrists, cardiologists, neurologists, oncologists, gastroenterologists, rheumatologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Psychiatrists, cardiologists, neurologists, oncologists, gastroenterologists, rheumatologists
Pitch tip
Test BEFORE the first dose: one result is valid for life.
UniPharmGx 19-Gene Comprehensive PGx Panel
What: One test that guides many medicines.
When: Mental health, heart, pain and more.
Sales pitch: "One test, valid for life, for many medicines."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · EDTA whole blood
MTHFR Mutations C677T / A1298C
What: MTHFR variants.
When: High homocysteine work-up, as the doctor advises.
Sales pitch: "5-day result when the doctor asks for it."
Code PTMP0089 · 5 days · PCR · EDTA whole blood
TPMT and NUDT15 Gene Analysis (Thiopurine toxicity)
What: TPMT and NUDT15 variants.
When: Before azathioprine or 6-mercaptopurine: avoids marrow suppression.
Sales pitch: "Prevents dangerous low blood counts."
Code PTMP0178 · 15 days · Sanger / PCR · EDTA whole blood
DPYD Gene Mutation Analysis (5-FU toxicity)
What: DPYD variants.
When: Before 5-FU or capecitabine chemo: avoids severe toxicity.
Sales pitch: "Test before the first dose of 5-FU."
Code PTMP0151 · 11 days · RT-PCR / Sanger · EDTA whole blood
