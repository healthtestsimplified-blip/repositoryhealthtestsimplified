Sales Guide: Endocrine, Metabolic & GI Genetics

Sales team test guide: 5 tests in Endocrine, Metabolic & GI Genetics. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Endocrinologists, diabetologists, gastroenterologists, paediatricians, pulmonologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Endocrinologists, diabetologists, gastroenterologists, paediatricians, pulmonologists
Pitch tip
Young diabetic with strong family history and no antibodies: think MODY.
Maturity Onset Diabetes of the Young (MODY) by NGS
What: MODY genes.
When: Diabetes at a young age, strong family history, antibody-negative.
Sales pitch: "Some MODY types need tablets, not insulin."
Code PTMP0216 · 21 days · NGS · EDTA whole blood
Hereditary Pancreatitis Panel
What: Pancreatitis genes.
When: Repeated or chronic pancreatitis at a young age.
Sales pitch: "Young recurrent pancreatitis deserves a genetic look."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · EDTA whole blood
Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CYP21A2 seq + MLPA)
What: CYP21A2 gene changes.
When: Salt-losing newborn, high 17-OHP, early puberty; carrier testing.
Sales pitch: "Confirms CAH and finds carriers."
Code PTMP0225 · 10 days · Sanger + MLPA · EDTA whole blood
Cystic Fibrosis Complete Gene Sequencing (CFTR)
What: Full CFTR gene sequencing.
When: Repeated chest infections, salty sweat, poor digestion; some male infertility.
Sales pitch: "Full gene, not just common mutations."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · EDTA whole blood
Gilbert Syndrome (UGT1A1 promoter mutation)
What: UGT1A1 promoter variant.
When: Mild, harmless jaundice; also before irinotecan chemo.
Sales pitch: "Reassures patients and guides irinotecan."
Code PTMP0155 · 10 days · Sanger · EDTA whole blood
