Sales Guide: Lung Cancer

Sales team test guide: 11 tests in Lung Cancer. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Medical oncologists, pulmonologists, thoracic surgeons, pathologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Medical oncologists, pulmonologists, thoracic surgeons, pathologists
Pitch tip
Every new non-small cell lung cancer should be tested BEFORE treatment starts.
EGFR Mutation Analysis (Exons 18-21)
What: Finds EGFR mutations in non-small cell lung cancer (adenocarcinoma).
When: Before starting EGFR tablets.
Sales pitch: "No EGFR result, no EGFR tablet."
Code PTMP0047 · 12 days · Sanger / Real time PCR · FFPE tumour block
EGFR Mutation by Real Time PCR
What: Quicker check of the common EGFR mutations.
When: When the oncologist needs a fast EGFR answer.
Sales pitch: "Common EGFR mutations in 9 days."
Code PTMP0021 · 9 days · Real time PCR · FFPE tumour block
Lung Cancer Panel: 12 Gene with PD-L1 (DAKO 22C3)
What: 12 key lung cancer genes plus PD-L1 for immunotherapy.
When: At diagnosis of advanced NSCLC: one-stop work-up.
Sales pitch: "Targeted drugs and immunotherapy answered from one block."
Code PTMP0153 · 22 days · NGS + IHC · FFPE tumour block
Lung Cancer Panel Liquid Biopsy, 12 Gene
What: The same key lung genes read from blood (ctDNA).
When: When tissue is too little or biopsy is not possible; tracking drug resistance.
Sales pitch: "No tissue? A blood sample can still guide targeted treatment."
Code PTMP0199 · 22 days · NGS · Streck blood (10 ml)
Lung Panel: EGFR Sequencing + ALK FISH + ROS1 FISH
What: The 3 key drivers (EGFR, ALK, ROS1) together.
When: At diagnosis of advanced NSCLC.
Sales pitch: "The three most important lung drivers in one order."
Code PTMP0141 · 10 days · Sanger + FISH · FFPE
ALK / EML4 Rearrangement by FISH
What: Confirms ALK-positive lung cancer.
When: Before ALK-targeted tablets.
Sales pitch: "ALK-positive patients get ALK tablets."
Code PTMP0043 · 9 days · FISH · FFPE tumour block
ROS-1 Rearrangement by FISH
What: Confirms ROS1-positive lung cancer.
When: Before ROS1-targeted tablets; to confirm a positive ROS1 IHC.
Sales pitch: "ROS1-positive patients get ROS1 tablets."
Code PTMP0044 · 9 days · FISH · FFPE tumour block
MET Gene Amplification by FISH
What: Detects MET amplification.
When: Resistance after EGFR tablets; MET-targeted options.
Sales pitch: "When EGFR tablets stop working, check MET."
Code PTMP0114 · 9 days · FISH · FFPE tumour block
PD-L1 SP263
What: Measures PD-L1 on the tumour.
When: To decide eligibility for immunotherapy.
Sales pitch: "Immunotherapy eligibility in 5 days."
Code PTHC0151 · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
ALK (D5F3) IHC
What: Fast screening for ALK.
When: When a quick ALK answer is needed.
Sales pitch: "ALK screening in 5 days."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
ROS1 (D4D6) IHC
What: Fast screening for ROS1; a positive result is confirmed by FISH.
When: As a quick first ROS1 check.
Sales pitch: "Screen fast; confirm positives by FISH."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
