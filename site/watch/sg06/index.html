Sales Guide: Neuro-Oncology

Sales team test guide: 10 tests in Neuro-Oncology. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Neurosurgeons, neuro-oncologists, radiation oncologists, neuropathologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Neurosurgeons, neuro-oncologists, radiation oncologists, neuropathologists
Pitch tip
WHO 2021 brain tumour classification needs these molecular tests for the final diagnosis.
CSF Cell-Free Tumour DNA
What: Reads tumour DNA in spinal fluid.
When: When a brain tumour biopsy is not possible.
Sales pitch: "A diagnosis option when the tumour cannot be biopsied."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · CSF
Histone Mutation Analysis (H3K27M, H3G34)
What: Histone mutations in gliomas.
When: Midline and diffuse hemispheric gliomas, often in children and young adults.
Sales pitch: "Needed for the WHO 2021 diagnosis."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · IHC / NGS · FFPE tumour block
KIAA1549 : BRAF Fusion (Pilocytic Astrocytoma)
What: Detects the KIAA1549-BRAF fusion.
When: To confirm pilocytic astrocytoma, mostly in children.
Sales pitch: "Confirms the diagnosis in childhood tumours."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · Break-apart FISH · FFPE tumour block
BRAF V600E Mutation Analysis
What: Detects BRAF V600E.
When: Some gliomas, where BRAF-targeted drugs are possible.
Sales pitch: "Opens a targeted option."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · IHC / Real time PCR / Sanger · FFPE tumour block
EGFR Amplification
What: EGFR amplification in brain tumours.
When: As a molecular feature of glioblastoma.
Sales pitch: "Part of the glioblastoma molecular picture."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · Dual FISH · FFPE tumour block
CDKN2A / 2B Homozygous Deletion
What: Detects CDKN2A/B deletion.
When: Grading IDH-mutant astrocytoma (grade 4 if deleted).
Sales pitch: "Changes the grade, so it changes treatment."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · Dual FISH · FFPE tumour block
IDH1 / IDH2 Sequencing (Adult Diffuse Glioma)
What: IDH status of gliomas.
When: Classifies adult glioma and predicts outlook.
Sales pitch: "The first molecular question in every adult glioma."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · Sanger · FFPE tumour block
TERT Gene Promoter Mutation Analysis
What: TERT promoter mutations.
When: Glioblastoma and oligodendroglioma classification.
Sales pitch: "Completes the WHO 2021 classification."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 15 days · Sanger · FFPE tumour block
MGMT Promoter Methylation Analysis
What: MGMT methylation status.
When: Glioblastoma: predicts benefit from temozolomide chemo.
Sales pitch: "Tells whether temozolomide is likely to help."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 15 days · RQ-PCR · FFPE tumour block
1p/19q Deletion by FISH (Oligodendroglioma)
What: 1p/19q co-deletion.
When: To confirm oligodendroglioma.
Sales pitch: "Confirms oligodendroglioma."
Code PTMP0195 · 12 days · Dual FISH · FFPE tumour block
