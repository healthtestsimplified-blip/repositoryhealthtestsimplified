Sales Guide: Haem-Onc: Broad NGS Panels

Sales team test guide: 4 tests in Haem-Onc: Broad NGS Panels. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Haematologists, haemato-oncologists, BMT physicians. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Haematologists, haemato-oncologists, BMT physicians
Pitch tip
Send fresh EDTA blood or bone marrow, not frozen. Attach the CBC / flow cytometry report.
Onco-Haem DNA Only Panel (40 genes)
What: Finds mutations such as FLT3, NPM1 and IDH in AML, MDS and MPN.
When: At diagnosis, for risk grouping and to choose targeted drugs.
Sales pitch: "One 40-gene test covers the key myeloid mutations for diagnosis, risk and targeted drugs."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 22 days · NGS · EDTA blood / bone marrow
Onco-Haem RNA Only Fusion Panel (29 genes)
What: Detects leukaemia fusions such as BCR-ABL1, PML-RARA and KMT2A.
When: When leukaemia is diagnosed or suspected and fusion status decides treatment.
Sales pitch: "Fusions change the treatment plan; this panel checks 29 of them together."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 22 days · NGS · EDTA blood / bone marrow
Comprehensive Myeloid & Lymphoid Panel
What: Broad work-up covering myeloid and lymphoid cancers (ALL, CLL, lymphoma).
When: When a blood cancer is suspected but the type is not yet clear.
Sales pitch: "When you are not sure if it is myeloid or lymphoid, one panel covers both."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 21-25 days · NGS · EDTA blood / bone marrow
Comprehensive Myeloid Panel (DNA + RNA fusions, 69 genes)
What: Complete AML / MDS work-up: mutations and fusions in one test.
When: At diagnosis of AML or MDS, for classification and risk.
Sales pitch: "Mutations plus fusions in one report: no need to send two samples."
Code PTMP0188 · 22 days · NGS · EDTA blood / bone marrow
