Sales Guide: Haemoglobinopathies & Bleeding Disorders

Sales team test guide: 4 tests in Haemoglobinopathies & Bleeding Disorders. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Haematologists, paediatricians, gynaecologists, IVF specialists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Haematologists, paediatricians, gynaecologists, IVF specialists
Pitch tip
HPLC first. Both partners carriers: DNA test of both, then CVS at 11-14 weeks.
Thalassemia v2 NGS
What: Complete alpha and beta globin genes in one test.
When: Complex cases.
Sales pitch: "Both globin genes in one test."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 21 days · NGS · EDTA whole blood
Alpha Thalassemia Mutation Analysis
What: Alpha globin gene changes.
When: Small red cells with normal HbA2 and iron; couples; prenatal.
Sales pitch: "Small red cells, normal HbA2 and iron: think alpha."
Code PTMP0105 · 15 days · Sanger + MLPA · Amniotic fluid / blood / CVS
Beta Thalassemia Mutation Analysis
What: Beta globin gene changes.
When: Carrier couple and prenatal diagnosis (CVS / amniocentesis).
Sales pitch: "Carrier couple: DNA test both, then prenatal (disease only, PCPNDT)."
Code PTMP0074 · 16 days · Sanger + MLPA · Amniotic fluid / blood / CVS
Hemophilia Complete Work-up (F8 / F9 seq + inversions)
What: F8 and F9 genes including inversions.
When: Haemophilia A or B: diagnosis and carrier testing of female relatives.
Sales pitch: "Finds carrier women in the family."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS + PCR · EDTA whole blood
