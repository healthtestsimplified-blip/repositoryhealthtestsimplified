Sales Guide: Breast, Ovarian & Hereditary Cancer

Sales team test guide: 11 tests in Breast, Ovarian & Hereditary Cancer. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Medical oncologists, breast surgeons, gynae-oncologists, gynaecologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Medical oncologists, breast surgeons, gynae-oncologists, gynaecologists
Pitch tip
ER/PR/HER2 for every breast cancer. HER2 IHC 2+ goes to HER2 FISH.
BRCA 1 & BRCA 2 Germline Sequencing
What: Finds inherited BRCA1/2 changes.
When: Breast or ovarian cancer patient, or strong family history; before PARP drugs; family testing.
Sales pitch: "One result helps the patient (PARP drugs) and her family."
Code PTMP0058 · 25 days · NGS · EDTA whole blood
BRCA 1 & BRCA 2 Somatic Mutation Test
What: Finds BRCA changes inside the tumour (e.g. ovarian cancer).
When: To decide PARP drug eligibility.
Sales pitch: "Tumour BRCA can open PARP drugs even when blood BRCA is normal."
Code PTMP0091 · 25 days · NGS · FFPE tumour block
BRCA1 & BRCA2 Advanced Panel (NGS + MLPA)
What: Most complete BRCA test: also finds large deletions.
When: When a full BRCA answer is needed.
Sales pitch: "Sequencing plus MLPA: deletions are not missed."
Code PTMP0152 · 23 days · NGS + MLPA · EDTA blood / FFPE
Breast Cancer Comprehensive Panel (BRCA 1 & 2) by NGS
What: BRCA plus other breast cancer genes.
When: Breast cancer patients or families needing more than BRCA.
Sales pitch: "More genes, 15 days."
Code PTMP0059 · 15 days · NGS · EDTA blood / FFPE
PIK3CA Mutation Screening
What: Finds PIK3CA mutations.
When: HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer: before a PIK3CA-targeted drug.
Sales pitch: "PIK3CA result decides the targeted drug."
Code PTMP0093 · 12 days · Real time PCR · FFPE tumour block
Homologous Recombination Deficiency (HRD Assay)
What: Shows who benefits from PARP drugs beyond BRCA.
When: Ovarian cancer, when planning maintenance therapy.
Sales pitch: "Finds PARP-eligible patients that BRCA alone misses."
Code PTMP0253 · 20 days · NGS + genomic scarring · FFPE tumour block
HRR Gene Panel - Germline
What: Homologous recombination repair genes.
When: Prostate, ovarian, breast and pancreatic cancer: for PARP drugs.
Sales pitch: "One panel across four cancers for PARP eligibility."
Code PTMP0129 · 15 days · NGS · EDTA whole blood
HER2/neu Amplification by FISH
What: Confirms HER2 status.
When: When HER2 IHC is 2+ (equivocal).
Sales pitch: "Every IHC 2+ needs FISH before deciding anti-HER2 therapy."
Code PTMP0003 · 18 days · FISH · FFPE tumour block
Hereditary Cancer Risk Panel
What: Checks many hereditary cancer syndromes.
When: Cancer at a young age, or many relatives with cancer.
Sales pitch: "One test for many family cancer syndromes."
Code PTMP0201 · 21 days · NGS · EDTA whole blood
Breast Panel - ER, PR, HER2, Ki67
What: Receptor status and growth rate.
When: Every new breast cancer: hormone therapy, anti-HER2, growth rate.
Sales pitch: "The basic panel every breast cancer needs, in 5 days."
Code PTHC0158 · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
Breast Panel - ER, PR, HER2
What: Basic receptor status of breast cancer.
When: Every new breast cancer.
Sales pitch: "Receptor status in 5 days."
Code PACK0009 · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
