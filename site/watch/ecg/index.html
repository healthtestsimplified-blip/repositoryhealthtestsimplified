ECG test: the heart tracing

Thursday tips, 8 Oct 2026. Chest pain, racing heart or dizziness? What an ECG shows and how to prepare.

What it is
An ECG (electrocardiogram) records the heart's tiny electrical signals as waves. Painless, takes only a few minutes, and no current goes into the body.
When doctors advise it
Chest pain or heaviness, fast or irregular heartbeat, dizziness or fainting, breathlessness, suspected heart attack. Also before an operation, after a past heart problem, with a strong family history of heart disease, or to check a pacemaker.
Preparation
Wear a top that opens easily. No cream, oil or powder on the chest. No exercise or cold water just before the test. Tell the staff about your medicines. Lie still and relax.
Reading the report
A resting heart rate of about 60 to 100 per minute with a regular rhythm is usually considered normal. An ECG records only a few seconds, so a normal ECG does not rule out every heart problem; your doctor may advise Holter, TMT or ECHO and reads the ECG with your symptoms.
Heart attack warning signs
Chest pain or pressure spreading to the arm, jaw or back, with sweating, breathlessness or uneasiness. Women, the elderly and people with diabetes may have no chest pain. Don't wait: call an ambulance or go to the nearest hospital.
