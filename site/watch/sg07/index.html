Sales Guide: Pan-Cancer, Liquid Biopsy & Pathology

Sales team test guide: 11 tests in Pan-Cancer, Liquid Biopsy & Pathology. For each test: what it checks, when doctors use it, the sample, report time, method and how to pitch it. Doctors to meet: Medical oncologists, pathologists, surgical oncologists. Always confirm MRP and code in the Rate Finder.

Doctors to meet
Medical oncologists, pathologists, surgical oncologists
Pitch tip
No tissue? Offer liquid biopsy. Need speed? Oncomine 50-gene panel in 7-10 days.
Solid Tumors Focus Fusion Panel (52 genes)
What: Gene fusions (ALK, ROS1, RET, NTRK...) across solid tumours.
When: Solid tumours where a fusion could open a targeted drug.
Sales pitch: "Fusion drugs work across cancer types; find the fusion first."
Code PTMP0221 · 20 days · NGS · FFPE tumour block
Oncocept Solid Single Gene (POLE)
What: POLE status.
When: Endometrial cancer molecular classification.
Sales pitch: "Needed to classify endometrial cancer."
Code PTMP0168 · 15 days · NGS · FFPE tumour block
Comprehensive Genomic Profiling (CGP / OCA Plus, 500+ genes)
What: 500+ cancer genes.
When: Advanced or rare cancers when standard options are exhausted.
Sales pitch: "When nothing else is left, look at every gene."
Code PTMP0181 · 35 days · Comprehensive NGS · FFPE tumour block
Liquid Biopsy Comprehensive Pan-Cancer Panel (cfDNA)
What: Cancer genes from blood.
When: When tissue is not available; monitoring and resistance.
Sales pitch: "Tumour genes from a blood sample."
Code PTMP0167 · 20 days · NGS · Streck blood (10 ml)
Liquid Biopsy 34 Genes (NGS)
What: Focused blood-based panel.
When: When a focused blood panel is enough.
Sales pitch: "A focused option from blood."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · Streck blood (10 ml)
Liquid Biopsy 104 Genes (NGS)
What: Broader blood-based panel.
When: When more genes are needed from blood.
Sales pitch: "Broader coverage from blood."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · ask lab · NGS · Streck blood (10 ml)
Oncomine Precision Assay (50 gene panel), tissue
What: Fast 50-gene solid tumour panel.
When: When speed matters.
Sales pitch: "50 genes in 7-10 days."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 7-10 days · NGS · FFPE tumour block
Oncomine Precision Assay (50 gene panel), blood
What: Same 50-gene panel from blood.
When: Fast results when tissue is not available.
Sales pitch: "The fast panel, from blood."
Code to confirm in the Rate Finder · 7-10 days · NGS · Streck blood (10 ml)
PDGFR Alpha (4q12) Rearrangement by FISH
What: PDGFRA rearrangement.
When: Blood cancers with high eosinophils.
Sales pitch: "Imatinib-responsive: worth finding."
Code PTMP0173 · 9 days · FISH · FFPE tumour block
Final Diagnosis Panel
What: Tumour typing by IHC.
When: When the histology is unclear.
Sales pitch: "Settles the diagnosis in 5 days."
Code PTHC0036 · 5 days · IHC (Ventana) · FFPE tumour block
IHC - Single Marker (any)
What: Any one marker the pathologist needs.
When: When a single extra marker is needed.
Sales pitch: "Any single marker, 5 days."
